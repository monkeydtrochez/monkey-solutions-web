## Accepted Deviations

These requirements deviate from the original spec. The deviation was reviewed and **explicitly accepted** by the owner on 2026-05-20. They are not gaps — the current implementation is the intentional final design.

### SKILLS-01 — Badge chip layout (accepted, not proficiency bars)

**Original spec:** "User sees a skills section with 4 groups (Languages, Frontend, Backend & Infra, Craft), each showing labeled 10-segment bars with filled segments indicating proficiency level"

**What was built:** `components/SkillsSection.tsx` renders `<Badge variant="outline">` chips grouped by category, backed by CMS (`profile.skillGroups` from GlobalContext). No proficiency segments.

**Why accepted:** Owner reviewed both approaches and rejected the proficiency bar implementation. The badge chip layout better fits the design aesthetic and avoids communicating rigid proficiency rankings. Current design is intentional.

---

### SVC-01 — 3-card services grid (accepted, not 4-card 2×2)

**Original spec:** "User sees a 2×2 grid of service cards, each with a title, description, and stack chip list"

**What was built:** `components/ServicesSection.tsx` has 3 service cards (Full-Stack Web, Dev advisory, Backend & APIs) in a `ms:grid-cols-3` layout.

**Why accepted:** Owner reviewed the layout and rejected adding a 4th card. The 3-service offering accurately represents current scope, and the 1×3 grid is the intentional final design.
